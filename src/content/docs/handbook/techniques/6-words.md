## Summary

Distill the issue to six words — forces clarity.

## When to use

5–10 min; fuzzy mandate; alignment check.

## Method

Each participant writes 6 words; compare; discuss gaps.

## RedTeam notes

Category: **analytical**. RedTeam may extend or alias this technique.
